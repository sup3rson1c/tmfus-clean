Put your address in and you come off every one of our email lists. No account, no login, no questions about why.

What happens next

Your address is recorded the moment you press the button. It is then removed from the sending platform, which is the step that actually stops mail leaving. We aim to have that done within 48 hours, and the law gives us 10 business days.

If something is already queued it can arrive during that window. After it, nothing should. If mail keeps coming a week later, that is a mistake rather than a policy, and replying to any message reaches a person who can fix it.

Other ways to stop the mail

  • Reply to any email we have sent with the word stop. A person reads it.
  • Use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any of our emails.

We do not sell or pass on an address once it has opted out. It stays on our suppression list purely so that nothing reaches it again.
