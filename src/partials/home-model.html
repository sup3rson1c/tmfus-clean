How we're paid

A brokerage, not a lender.

TMF Team prepares your file, presents it to funders and lenders, and manages the process. Every credit decision, and every rate, amount and term, belongs to the funder.

TMF Team is paid a commission by the funder when a transaction funds, not by you.
