Who we fund.

A cash injection is underwritten mainly on revenue and the health of your bank statements, so businesses with imperfect personal credit are regularly funded. The score affects pricing more than eligibility.

  • Restaurants & cafés Daily card and cash sales
  • Retail Point-of-sale revenue
  • Construction Project and progress billing
  • Trucking Load-based receivables
  • Healthcare Insurance and patient receivables
  • Wholesale Inventory-heavy cash cycles
  • Salons & spas Appointment and retail revenue
  • E-commerce Payment processor payouts

We also work with auto services, beauty, professional services, manufacturing, real estate, technology, staffing, cleaning, landscaping, entertainment, education and fitness businesses.
