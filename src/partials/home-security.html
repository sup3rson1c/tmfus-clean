Your file, handled like a vault.

Bank statements and personal details are the most sensitive documents a business owner shares. This is how they're protected.

  • 256-bit encrypted connection

    Every form on this site, from the calculator to the application, runs over an encrypted connection.

  • Sensitive details are encrypted before storage

    Your Social Security number and signature are entered in the application and encrypted as soon as they reach us. They're never sent by email or stored in plain text.

  • No hard pull to look

    The calculator uses the credit band you choose. A credit report is only pulled later, with your written authorization, once an application is being underwritten.
