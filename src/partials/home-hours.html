Where the hours go.

From form to funding in one day, in three stages, designed to be precise, fast and free of friction.

    Four short steps, about ten minutes, with four months of business bank statements. See what you qualify for first if you like: no login and no credit pull.

    Review and offers

    Funders review your file and send their offers. A funding specialist walks you through each one: amount, cost and term, side by side.

    Get funded

    Accept an offer and the money is usually in your account within a couple of hours, and within 4–24 hours of applying. SBA loans run on their own 30–90 day track.
