Four ways to fund.

From short-term cash injection to long-term structured loans. Each path is priced by its own funders and compared here on the same terms: how much, for how long, how soon, and who it suits.

ProductRangeTermDecisionBest for
  • Cash injectionMCA Range: $5K–$2M Term: 3–18 months Decision: 3–24 hours Best for: Steady revenue, imperfect credit, capital needed fast
  • SBA loans7(a) · 504 Range: Up to $5.5M Term: 10–25 years Decision: 30–90 days Best for: The lowest rates, if you can wait for them
  • Home equityHELOC Range: $25K–$750K Term: 10–30 years Decision: Offer inside the hour Best for: Owners with home equity and time for a cheaper rate
  • Line of creditRevolving Range: $10K–$250K Term: Revolving Decision: Varies by funder Best for: Drawing what you need, and paying only for what you use

Ranges and terms are indicative and set by each funder after underwriting.
