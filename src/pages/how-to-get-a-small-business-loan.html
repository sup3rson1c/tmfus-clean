How do you get a small business loan?

Six steps from "we need capital" to money in the account, and the documents each type of lender will ask for.

By TMF Team Last updated 25 October 2026 Reading time 5 minutes

Short answer

Decide how much you need and what for, check what you qualify for without a credit pull, gather your documents (at minimum your EIN and four months of business bank statements), pick the product that fits your deadline, apply once, and compare every offer in writing before you sign. A broker can put one application in front of several funders.

The six steps

  1. Name the amount and the purpose. Lenders price risk by use: payroll, inventory, equipment and property each point to a different product.
  2. Check what you qualify for. The funding calculator gives an indicative range in about a minute and never touches your credit file.
  3. Gather your documents. See the checklist below.
  4. Match the product to the deadline. SBA loans take 30 to 90 days; a merchant cash advance can fund within 4 to 24 hours of applying.
  5. Apply once. The TMF Team application is four steps and takes about four minutes.
  6. Compare offers in writing. The rate, amount and term come from the funder before you sign. Compare the total cost, not just the payment.

What documents you need

ProductWhat you will be asked for
Merchant cash advanceBusiness details, EIN, owner details, four months of business bank statements
Line of creditBusiness details and four months of business bank statements; a funder may run a credit check before a final offer
Home equity lineEIN, four months of business bank statements, the property address; income, title and property verified after the offer
SBA 7(a) or 504Usually three years of business and personal tax returns, a year-to-date profit and loss and balance sheet, bank statements, a personal financial statement for each 20% owner, a debt schedule, use of proceeds, licences and entity documents, and a personal guarantee

Does applying hurt your credit?

Checking what you qualify for with the calculator does not. A credit report is only pulled with your written authorization, once an application is being underwritten.

Mistakes that slow you down

  • Applying for an SBA loan when the need is this week.
  • Sending personal bank statements instead of the business account.
  • Leaving out open advances. Funders will see them in the statements.
  • Comparing offers on the payment alone instead of the total cost.

Common questions.

It depends on the product. A merchant cash advance usually funds within 4 to 24 hours of applying. An SBA loan commonly takes 30 to 90 days.

It depends on the product and the funder. Revenue-based funding needs four months of business bank statements to underwrite. SBA 504 asks for a larger down payment, 15% instead of 10%, when the business is under two years old.

Not for every product. SBA loans require a personal guarantee from principal owners and lenders often ask for collateral as well. A merchant cash advance is underwritten on revenue. A home equity line is secured on your home.
