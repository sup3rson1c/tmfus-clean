About the calculator.

It takes your monthly revenue, adjusts it for your industry and your credit band, subtracts what you still owe on any existing advances, and shows the result as a range of plus or minus 15 percent. It is deliberately simple, and it is the same arithmetic a funder starts from before looking at your bank statements.
