Why a line of credit

One approval. Capital whenever the business needs it.

A HELOC is a revolving line, not a one-time loan. You get approved once, draw what you need, pay it down, and draw again during the draw period. You don't need a new application each time.

  • Draw only what you need

    Take part of the line now and keep the rest ready for the next opportunity or the next slow month.

  • Pay interest on what you use

    Interest is charged on the balance you have drawn, not on the full limit sitting in reserve.

  • Pay it down, draw it again

    As you repay, that room opens back up during the draw period. Same line, same approval.

  • Long terms, smaller payments

    10 to 30 years instead of the weeks or months on a cash advance, so the payment fits your cash flow.

  • Usually cheaper than unsecured credit

    Secured by your property equity, so the rate typically sits well below unsecured business funding.

  • Built for how a business spends

    Payroll, inventory, equipment, a seasonal dip, or clearing expensive advances, all from one line.

How your offer is chosen

AI does the valuing, the checking and the choosing.

From the moment you press the button, AI systems run every step. They value the property, check the numbers, and pick the best offer the lender can make you. That is why the answer comes back in minutes instead of days.

  1. 01First minute

    AI estimates what the property is worth

    Automated valuation models read the property record and recent sales of comparable properties nearby, then work out a current market value. No appraiser, no visit.

  2. 02Next

    AI checks the numbers

    It sets that value against the mortgages and liens already on the property to find your usable equity, and reads a soft credit check (no effect on your score) alongside your revenue.

  3. 03A few minutes

    AI picks the best offer

    From everything the lender can offer on those numbers, it picks the best amount and rate and shows them on this page, with every term side by side. A no comes back just as fast as a yes.
