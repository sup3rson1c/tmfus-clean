How fast can a business get funded?

The honest timeline for each type of funding, and the few things that decide whether you land at the quick end.

By TMF Team Last updated 25 October 2026 Reading time 3 minutes

Short answer

With a merchant cash advance, offers typically arrive within 2 to 4 hours of a complete application and funding usually lands within 4 to 24 hours of applying, often a couple of hours after you accept. A home equity indication comes inside the hour in business hours. SBA loans take 30 to 90 days.

Other products

  • Home equity line: an indicative offer inside the hour, Monday to Friday, 9am to 6pm EST, from an automated valuation. Income, title and property verification come after, and final terms can move.
  • Line of credit: depends on the funder. Your offer says how a draw is requested and how long it takes to reach your account.
  • SBA 7(a) and 504: commonly 30 to 90 days from a complete application, because of the paperwork and the government guarantee.

What slows funding down

  • An incomplete file. The clock starts when funders have everything, including four months of business bank statements.
  • Statements from a personal account instead of the business account.
  • Open advances the application does not mention. Funders will see them in the statements.
  • Choosing a product that does not fit the deadline. If you need capital this week, an SBA loan is the wrong instrument.
