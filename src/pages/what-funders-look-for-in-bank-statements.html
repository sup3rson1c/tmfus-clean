What do funders look for in four months of bank statements?

Bank statements are the heart of a revenue-based funding file. Here is why they are required and what an underwriter reads in them.

By TMF Team Last updated 25 October 2026 Reading time 4 minutes

Short answer

Funders use four months of business bank statements to see real revenue, how steady it is, what balance the account keeps, and what you already owe. For a merchant cash advance they matter more than your credit score, because the advance is repaid from that revenue. Four months are required in every state.

Why bank statements, not tax returns

A merchant cash advance is repaid as a share of your revenue, so the funder needs to see that revenue as it actually lands. Bank statements show it month by month. Tax returns describe last year; statements show the business as it is now.

SBA lending is different and much more document-heavy: expect tax returns, a profit and loss statement, a balance sheet and more, alongside bank statements.

What an underwriter reads in them

  • Monthly deposits. The revenue the business really brings in, which sets the starting point for how much can be advanced.
  • Consistency. Whether deposits are steady or swing hard from month to month.
  • Balances. Whether the account keeps a cushion or runs close to zero.
  • Existing positions. Regular debits to other funders. Every open advance reduces what is left to advance against.
  • Overdrafts and returned items. Signs the account is under strain.

How the estimate uses the same numbers

The funding calculator starts where a funder starts: monthly revenue, adjusted for industry and credit band, minus what you still owe on existing advances, shown as a range of plus or minus 15 percent. The bank statements are what turn that projection into a real offer, which can land above or below the range.

How to prepare

  • Download the four most recent complete months as PDFs straight from your bank.
  • Use the business account, not a personal one.
  • Know what you owe on any open advances, because the funder will see it anyway.
  • Have your EIN, business start date and ownership split ready. The full application takes about four minutes.

How your statements are protected

Statements are attached inside the application. Your Social Security number, date of birth and signature are encrypted on arrival with a key the web server does not hold, and are never emailed or stored in plain text. Do not send any of them in the chat.
