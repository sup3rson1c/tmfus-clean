Can you get business funding with bad credit?

Often, yes. It depends far more on which product you apply for than on the score itself.

By TMF Team Last updated 25 October 2026 Reading time 4 minutes

Short answer

Often yes. There is no single credit cutoff. A merchant cash advance is underwritten mainly on revenue and bank statement health, so businesses with imperfect personal credit are funded regularly; the score changes the price more than the answer. A home equity line is the opposite: it is priced off your score.

How much credit matters, product by product

ProductHow much your credit score matters
Merchant cash advanceLeast. Priced and approved mainly on revenue and bank statements.
Line of creditVaries by funder. A funder may run a credit check before a final offer.
Home equity lineA lot. The rate improves at roughly 660, 700, 740 and 780.
SBA loanA lot. Lenders add their own credit standards on top of the SBA's floor.

What matters more than the score

For revenue-based funding, the underwriter cares most about what your bank statements show: steady deposits, a healthy balance, and how much you already owe to other funders. See what funders look for in bank statements.

Checking without hurting your credit

Running the funding calculator never touches your credit file; it uses the credit band you choose yourself. A credit report is only pulled later, with your written authorization, once an application is being underwritten.

When the honest answer is "not another advance"

If the balance you still owe on existing advances is larger than your revenue supports, a new advance is the wrong tool. That is not a decline. It means the conversation should be about consolidation or a payoff structure, and that conversation is usually worth having.
