# AI-search visibility plan for tmfus.com (22 Oct 2026)

Source: Julian Goldie video summaries (GEO / "query fanout" idea). Treat his statistics as unverified.

## Already in place (audited 22 Oct)

- robots.txt explicitly allows every AI crawler (GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended, Applebot and more).
- llms.txt with product facts and "notes for assistants".
- JSON-LD on every page: WebSite, WebPage, BreadcrumbList, Service, LoanOrCredit, FAQPage (5 to 9 questions on the product pages), PostalAddress.
- Sitemap generated by build.mjs.

## Added in this pass

- `llms-full.txt`: every FAQ question and answer, generated from the pages' own JSON-LD (`node tools/gen-llms-full.mjs`, run after `node build.mjs`). Listed in `.cpanel.yml` and checked by `verify.sh`.
- `llms.txt` date refreshed and linked to the full file; robots.txt comment points at both.

## Query-fanout content gaps (candidate pages, each needs John's approval of the wording)

When someone asks an assistant "how do I get business funding fast", the assistant also searches sub-questions. Pages that answer these plainly, in the site's frozen facts, are the ones that get quoted:

- What is a merchant cash advance and how is it repaid?
- MCA vs SBA loan vs line of credit: which fits which business?
- What do funders look at in four months of bank statements?
- Can I get business funding with bad personal credit?
- How fast can a business get funded (2 to 4 hours for offers, 4 to 24 hours to fund)?
- Is a broker different from a lender? What does a broker cost?
- HELOC for business owners: how it works, who qualifies.

Build them only after launch is approved ("launch website" is John's call), as plain question-led pages that reuse the existing facts and disclaimers.

## Measure

After launch, ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Claude the questions above monthly and note whether "TMF Team" or tmfus.com is cited. Google Search Console shows the query list.
