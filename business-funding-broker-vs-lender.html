Is a business funding broker different from a lender?

Yes, and the difference changes who decides, who pays, and how many offers you see.

By TMF Team Last updated 25 October 2026 Reading time 3 minutes

Short answer

A lender decides and funds. A broker like TMF Team prepares your file, presents it to several funders and lenders, and manages the process, but every credit decision, rate, amount and term belongs to the funder. TMF Team is paid a commission by the funder when a deal funds, not by you, and it is not added to your balance.

Who pays itThe funder, by commission, when a deal fundsYou, through the cost of the funding

Why use a broker

Because the price is set by the funder after underwriting, putting your file in front of several funders matters more than any rate quoted upfront. One application replaces applying to lenders one at a time, and a specialist can tell you when a product is the wrong fit, for example when an SBA loan cannot meet your deadline or when another advance would do more harm than good.

What to ask any broker

  • Are you a lender or a broker?
  • Who pays you, and is your fee added to my balance?
  • Will I see every cost in writing from the funder before I sign?
  • Who will my information be shared with?

How your information is shared

With your express written consent, applicant information is shared with funders, lenders, other brokers and marketing partners, and TMF Team may be paid for it. You can opt out at any time on the privacy page. The full detail is in the terms and privacy policy.
