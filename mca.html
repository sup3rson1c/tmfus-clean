How remittance works

Payments that follow your sales.

Remittances are a share of revenue. Higher revenue days mean faster payoff; slower days mean lower remittances.

There's no rigid maturity date. Payoff timing adjusts with how the business actually performs.

Know what it costs

Not a loan.

A cash injection is a purchase of future receivables. Here's what that means for how it's priced and repaid.

Pricing
A factor rate applied to the advance, not an interest rate. Your offer shows the total cost of capital before you accept.
Remittance
A percentage of revenue, collected daily or weekly. Because it's percentage-based, it falls alongside revenue.
Maturity
No rigid maturity date. Payoff timing adjusts with business performance.
Credit
Underwritten mainly on revenue and bank statement health. The score affects pricing more than eligibility.

Terms depend on underwriting, your bank statements and the funder. Not available in every state.
