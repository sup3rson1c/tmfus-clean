1. Who this applies to

This policy covers tmfus.com and everything TMF Team does with information collected through it — the calculators, the chat, the contact form, the funding application, and email or telephone contact that follows from any of them. It applies to business owners, applicants, guarantors, and anybody whose details are submitted to us by somebody else.

It does not cover what a funder, lender or other partner does with your information after we pass it on. Once your file reaches them, their own privacy policy governs. We will tell you who has it if you ask.

3. Where it comes from

  • From you — forms, calculators, chat, the application, uploaded documents, email and telephone calls.
  • Automatically — from your browser and from cookies, as described in section 6.
  • From other people — a co-owner, partner or accountant may give us your details in connection with an application. If they do, we rely on their confirmation that you agreed.
  • From third parties — credit bureaus, identity and bank verification services, business data providers, funders reporting the status of your file, and marketing partners or lead sources that refer you to us.

4. How we use it

  • To produce the estimate you asked for and to match you to funding products.
  • To take, complete and place a funding application, and to manage it through to funding.
  • To verify who you are, to assess creditworthiness, and to meet know-your-customer, anti-money-laundering, anti-fraud and other legal obligations.
  • To contact you about your enquiry or application by phone, text and email.
  • To market funding and related business products and services to you, including through advertising on other websites and platforms.
  • To keep a record of the consents and authorizations you gave, and of the exact wording you agreed to.
  • To run, secure, measure and improve the website and our service.
  • To resolve disputes, enforce our terms, and comply with the law.

We use your Social Security number, date of birth and signature to underwrite, verify your identity, document the agreement, and place, syndicate, sell, service or collect the transaction — and we share them with the parties in section 5 for those purposes. We do not use them to target advertising at you.

5. Who we share it with

Sharing is how brokering works. To get you an offer we have to show your file to people who write offers. When you use this site and when you submit an application, you authorize us to disclose, transfer, sell, license and otherwise share your information with:

  • Funders and lenders — funding companies, banks, credit unions, factoring companies and equipment lessors, so they can underwrite you and make an offer.
  • The wider commercial finance industry — other brokers, independent sales organizations, syndicators, participants, buyers of receivables, servicers, collection agents and referral partners, so your file can reach funders we do not deal with directly, so a transaction can be syndicated, sold, serviced or collected, and so you can be offered products we do not broker ourselves.
  • Marketing, advertising and lead partners — so that funding and related business products can be marketed to you, including through advertising platforms that match your details to your account with them.
  • Service providers — hosting, storage, email, telephony, live chat, e-signature, analytics, document processing and support, each acting on our instructions.
  • Credit bureaus and verification services — to obtain reports and to verify identity, bank accounts and businesses.
  • Our affiliates and related companies.
  • Professional advisers, insurers, auditors, regulators, courts and law enforcement — where we are required to, or where we need to establish or defend a legal claim.
  • A buyer or investor — if TMF Team is merged, acquired, financed or sold, your information is part of what transfers.

We may be paid for this. We receive commissions from funders when a transaction funds, and we may receive fees or other consideration for a referral or for a lead. Under California law, disclosing personal information for consideration is a “sale”, and disclosing it for cross-context behavioural advertising is “sharing”. We do both, and we say so plainly here rather than hiding it. You can stop it — see Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information.

What we share includes sensitive personal information. Your Social Security number, your date of birth and your electronic signature are part of the file that goes to funders, lenders, other brokers, syndicators, buyers, servicers and verification services, so that your application can be underwritten, funded, syndicated, sold, serviced or collected, and so that your identity can be verified. We may be paid in connection with those disclosures. You authorize this when you sign the application, and you can withdraw the authorization — see section 7.

What we do not do with it. We do not include your Social Security number, date of birth or signature in an advertising audience, and we do not supply them to an advertising platform, a general data broker, or anyone with no role in a funding transaction. Those three fields travel only to parties actually working on a transaction, and they stay encrypted on our side the whole time they sit with us — see section 12.

A current list of the partners we are actively sharing with is available on request from privacy@tmfus.com.

Your browser can help too. Every major browser lets you block or delete cookies in its settings. Blocking necessary cookies will break parts of this site, including the chat and the cookie banner itself.

9. California privacy rights

If you live in California, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA gives you these rights.

  • To know what personal information we collected about you, where it came from, why we collected it, and who we disclosed it to.
  • To a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • To correct personal information that is wrong.
  • To delete personal information we hold, subject to the exceptions below.
  • To opt out of the sale and sharing of your personal information — section 8.
  • To limit the use of sensitive personal information. We use Social Security numbers, dates of birth and signatures to underwrite, verify and document your application and to place, syndicate, sell, service or collect the transaction — uses that are necessary to provide the service you asked us for. We do not use them to infer characteristics about you and we do not use them for advertising. If you want them restricted further, email privacy@tmfus.com and we will tell you what that means for a live application.
  • Not to be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights. We will not deny you service, charge you more, or give you a worse product because you asked.

What we sold or shared in the last twelve months: identifiers, commercial information, financial information, credit information, internet activity and sensitive personal information, disclosed to funders, lenders, other brokers, syndicators, buyers, servicers, verification services, and marketing and advertising partners, as described in section 5. Sensitive personal information — Social Security numbers, dates of birth and signatures — went only to parties working on a funding transaction, never to an advertising partner or a general data broker. We do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16.

How to make a request. Email privacy@tmfus.com, or use the form in section 8, or write to us at the address in section 15. Deletion has limits. 11. Financial privacy notice

Because we arrange financing, some of what we hold is protected by the federal Gramm-Leach-Bliley Act. Under it:

  • We collect nonpublic personal information from your applications, your transactions, your bank statements and consumer reporting agencies.
  • We disclose it to nonaffiliated third parties — funders, lenders, other brokers, service providers and marketing partners — as permitted by law and as described in section 5.
  • You have the right to limit disclosures to nonaffiliated third parties for their own marketing purposes. To exercise it, email privacy@tmfus.com with the words “limit sharing”. It does not stop us sending your file to funders in order to place an application you have asked us to place.
  • We restrict access to your information to the people who need it, and we maintain physical, electronic and procedural safeguards to protect it.

12. How we protect information

The application is built so that the most sensitive part of it cannot be read even by somebody who takes the whole server.

  • The site is served over HTTPS. Nothing personal is ever put in a web address, because addresses end up in logs and browser history.
  • Social Security numbers, dates of birth and signature images are encrypted the instant they arrive, with a public key held on the server and a private key that is not and never will be. They are decrypted only on an authorized device, never on the server.
  • Those fields are blocked, independently and in four different places, from reaching an email, a spreadsheet, a chat transcript or any plain-text file.
  • Bank statements are stored outside the public website folder, are not reachable from the internet, and are readable only by the account that runs the site.
  • The inbox where applications are read is password protected and does not itself have the ability to decrypt anything.

No system is perfect and we cannot promise absolute security. If a breach affects your information we will tell you and the relevant regulators as the law requires.

13. How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it, and then for as long as the law requires. In practice: an application and its documents are kept for the life of any resulting funding plus the record-keeping period the law imposes; an enquiry that never becomes an application is kept while it is still live commercially; and chat transcripts and website analytics are kept for a shorter period. You can ask us to delete your information sooner — see section 9.

14. Children

This site is for business owners. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has given us information, write to privacy@tmfus.com and we will delete it.

15. Changes, and how to reach us

We may update this policy. The version and date at the top of this page always show the current one, and a material change will be made obvious on the site. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept it.
